Why MemoCare

Linked reminder chains

Some doses only make sense after another step (for example after a meal). Connect related reminders so confirming one step unlocks the next — fewer missed doses than a pile of unrelated alarms.

Escalating alerts

If you don't respond, reminders can escalate from a gentle notification to sound, vibration, and a full-screen alarm with large actions — designed with accessibility in mind.

Caregiver awareness

Optional caregiver contact so family can be notified when a dose is missed (for example via WhatsApp where you configure it).

Hydration & today's schedule

Track water intake and see today's schedule on a simple home dashboard.

Offline-first

Your reminders and history stay on your phone. No account is required for core scheduling.

Open source

Inspect the code, suggest improvements, and self-host your own builds from the public repository.

Who it's for

Families & caregivers

Supporting parents or grandparents with multiple medications and meal-linked timing.

Busy adults

Anyone who needs unmistakable alarms until they tap Done, Snooze, or Skip.

Important MemoCare is a support tool, not a medical device. It does not replace professional medical advice. Always follow your doctor or pharmacist's instructions. See Terms of Service for limitations of liability.
