Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

Introduction

MemoCare ("the App") helps you manage medication and related reminders on your device. This policy describes how information is handled. Replace this paragraph with your legal entity name and contact email before publishing to the Play Store, if required.

Data stored on your device

Reminders, confirmation history, hydration counts, settings (including optional caregiver contact details you enter), and similar data are stored locally on your phone using the app's database. This data is not sent to MemoCare servers by the app itself, because the open-source build does not require a login or central account.

Permissions

The App may request permissions such as notifications, exact alarms, full-screen alerts, vibration, boot completion (to reschedule alarms), internet (e.g. to open caregiver links like WhatsApp), and phone (for optional call shortcuts). Grant only what you are comfortable with; core scheduling is designed to work offline.

Third-party services

When you choose to open a link (for example a messaging app for caregiver alerts), that service is governed by its own privacy policy. MemoCare does not control third-party apps.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 for collection of personal information. Parents or guardians should supervise use for minors.

Your choices

You can clear all app data or uninstall the App at any time from your device settings. This removes locally stored information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will change; continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
